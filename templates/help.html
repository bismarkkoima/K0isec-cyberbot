🆘 I think I've been scammed

Take a deep breath. It is not your fault - scammers are professionals who trick thousands of people every day. Acting quickly can help.

  1. Call your bank now using the phone number on the back of your bank card. Tell them what happened and ask them to stop any payments.
  2. Change your passwords, starting with your email, then your bank and shopping accounts.
  3. If someone connected to your computer, turn off the Wi-Fi or unplug the internet cable, and ask a trusted person or repair shop to check it.
  4. Report it to the police or your national fraud reporting service. Keep any messages, phone numbers and receipts.
  5. Tell someone you trust - a family member, friend or neighbour. You don't have to deal with this alone.
  6. Watch out for "recovery" offers. Anyone promising to get your money back for a fee is usually another scammer.
